## Empty, loading, stale, and error rule

- Empty means a confirmed zero and offers the next valid action.
- Loading states show known deployment facts or a saved public snapshot rather than replacing the page with placeholders.
- Stale states keep the last successful values, observation time, and a Retry action.
- Errors never expose RPC messages, credentials, worker internals, or infer a healthy/zero state.
