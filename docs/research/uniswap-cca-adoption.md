# Uniswap CCA adoption for ORBIT 4444

## Assessment

Adopt a complete CCA flow for the Base Sepolia hackathon build only if the team is willing to change the present genesis economics. The deployed Uniswap `LBPStrategy` can migrate into ORBIT's custom v4 hook, and CCA can raise WETH, but the stock path cannot preserve the current invariant that all 4,444 FUEL begin in a WETH-free, one-sided, permanently locked genesis position. It requires a nonzero auction allocation, a nonzero reserved LP allocation smaller than the distributed amount, and a positive share of raised currency for LP. Those constraints make CCA a token sale followed by two-sided liquidity, not merely price discovery for the existing genesis construction ([`LBPStrategy.initializeDistribution`](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/873cbb23c5019a795193c5ad561edff2f78ba5a3/src/strategies/lbp/LBPStrategy.sol#L60-L141), [`MigratorParams.validate`](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/873cbb23c5019a795193c5ad561edff2f78ba5a3/src/libraries/MigratorParams.sol#L85-L130)).

CCA has no published prize advantage. ETHOnline 2026 explicitly accepts “any part of the Uniswap stack,” listing CCA alongside the API and v2/v3/v4. The published qualification requirements are a public open-source repository, `FEEDBACK.md`, and the feedback-form submission; they assign no extra score or weighting to CCA ([ETHGlobal prize page](https://ethglobal.com/events/ethonline2026/prizes)). CCA is therefore worthwhile only if the end-to-end auction improves the submission, not as a presumed judging multiplier.

## Verified stock behavior

### Launcher custody and distribution

For an existing token, the intended call is one `LiquidityLauncher.multicall`: optional Permit2 permit, `depositToken`, then `distributeToken`. The launcher temporarily holds the tokens, gives the chosen strategy an allowance, and requires the strategy to consume the full allowance. Tokens left in the launcher between transactions can be distributed by anyone, so splitting these calls is unsafe ([deployment guide](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/1eda9f0c0243e2fdc0cbe0d665200ffa8c2ba53a/docs/DeploymentGuide.md#token-distribution), [`LiquidityLauncher`](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/1eda9f0c0243e2fdc0cbe0d665200ffa8c2ba53a/src/LiquidityLauncher.sol)).

The stock LBP strategy transfers `totalSupply - reservedTokenAmountForLP` directly from the launcher to the new CCA and holds the reserved portion itself until migration. The launched token must be an ERC-20, cannot be fee-on-transfer, and `reservedTokenAmountForLP` must be greater than zero and strictly smaller than the distributed amount ([deployed `LBPStrategy`](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/873cbb23c5019a795193c5ad561edff2f78ba5a3/src/strategies/lbp/LBPStrategy.sol#L60-L141)).

This has a repo-specific consequence: before `FuelCore.launch()`, a FUEL transfer succeeds only when both endpoints are discovery-exempt, and exemption changes freeze at launch. The launcher, LBP strategy, predicted CCA, and every FUEL recovery/claim custodian therefore need exemptions before distribution; the deterministic CCA address should be predicted before the exemption transaction ([`FuelCore`](https://github.com/hrsh22/ethonline-26/blob/e6528f85b0bb25ae41c96fca09e027ef4f462099/packages/contracts/src/FuelCore.sol#L181-L205), [pre-launch transfer rule](https://github.com/hrsh22/ethonline-26/blob/e6528f85b0bb25ae41c96fca09e027ef4f462099/packages/contracts/src/FuelCore.sol#L452-L502)).

### Bid ownership, refunds, and claims

CCA accepts native ETH when `currency == address(0)`, or an ERC-20 pulled from the transaction sender through Permit2. `submitBid` separately accepts an `owner`; the payer and owner need not be the same ([CCA `submitBid`](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/a56d42231e7bf048136d9d88fa61e8518c10c5ff/src/ContinuousClearingAuction.sol#L462-L492)). The optional bid-validation hook receives both `owner` and `sender`, so it can enforce a safe relation between them ([`IValidationHook`](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/a56d42231e7bf048136d9d88fa61e8518c10c5ff/src/interfaces/IValidationHook.sol)).

After the auction, a bid must first be exited. A failed auction gives the bid owner a full refund and zero tokens. A graduated auction records the filled tokens and returns the unspent currency to the owner. Later, anyone may call `claimTokens`, but the complete fill is always transferred to the stored owner; `claimTokensBatch` aggregates fills for one owner into one transfer. Neither API supports a bounded partial token claim ([exit logic](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/a56d42231e7bf048136d9d88fa61e8518c10c5ff/src/ContinuousClearingAuction.sol#L494-L600), [claim logic](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/a56d42231e7bf048136d9d88fa61e8518c10c5ff/src/ContinuousClearingAuction.sol#L603-L659)).

Direct claims are therefore incompatible with a winning ordinary wallet gaining more than 64 whole FUEL in one transfer: `FuelCore` reverts when lost plus gained whole units exceed 64 ([mutation cap](https://github.com/hrsh22/ethonline-26/blob/e6528f85b0bb25ae41c96fca09e027ef4f462099/packages/contracts/src/FuelCore.sol#L479-L502)). The viable adapter is a deterministic, pre-launch-exempt bidder escrow as the CCA bid owner. It must attribute both refunds and token fills to the funding user, then release FUEL in transfers that each cause at most 64 mutations. The bid-validation hook should reject arbitrary owner addresses and prove the approved escrow/sender relation; otherwise a payer can direct both refund and FUEL rights to an unintended address. This escrow is ORBIT-specific code, not stock CCA behavior, and requires adversarial tests for ownership, replay, partial release, refund withdrawal, and failed-auction accounting.

Per-bidder escrows may be created while the auction runs only if FUEL remains unlaunched: each predicted/deployed escrow must be registered, discovery-exempt, and protected before it can receive claimed FUEL, and those flags cannot be added after launch. The validation hook must reject unregistered owners so the public `owner` parameter cannot bypass that preparation.

### Failure and unsold balances

On failure to reach `requiredCurrencyRaised`, all bids are refundable, `sweepCurrency` transfers zero, and `tokensRecipient` can sweep the full auction supply. On graduation, `tokensRecipient` can separately sweep only the unsold remainder ([currency and unsold sweeps](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/a56d42231e7bf048136d9d88fa61e8518c10c5ff/src/ContinuousClearingAuction.sol#L661-L699), [`AuctionStorage`](https://github.com/Uniswap/continuous-clearing-auction/blob/a56d42231e7bf048136d9d88fa61e8518c10c5ff/src/AuctionStorage.sol#L87-L106)). Stock LBP requires its own address to be the CCA `fundsRecipient` and forbids itself as `tokensRecipient`; unsold FUEL therefore needs an explicit, exempt recipient with a documented burn, bounded-distribution, or recovery policy ([initializer validation](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/873cbb23c5019a795193c5ad561edff2f78ba5a3/src/strategies/lbp/LBPStrategy.sol#L357-L379)).

Migration is permissionless after `migrationBlock` and one-shot. It sweeps only settled raised currency, initializes the committed pool at the final auction price, mints configured positions, sends LP NFTs to `positionRecipient` or per-position overrides, and sends unused assets to `recipient`. Unsold auction tokens remain in the CCA. If pool initialization or position creation fails, the strategy consumes the reservation, returns raised currency and reserved FUEL to `recipient`, creates no LP, and cannot retry that initializer ([migration implementation](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/873cbb23c5019a795193c5ad561edff2f78ba5a3/src/strategies/lbp/LBPStrategy.sol#L146-L265)). Recovery endpoints must therefore be exempt and operational before `FuelCore.launch()`.

In the stock path, the CCA will release settled proceeds only to the immutable LBP strategy, and that strategy exposes no creator/operator sweep before migration; audit this invariant again if a custom strategy is introduced. A custom strategy must not give an operator a path to remove auction proceeds before either successful pool initialization or the documented terminal recovery branch.

### Post-auction v4 hook

The deployed stock strategy does support a custom post-auction hook; `hook = address(0)` is not mandatory. A nonzero hook must be a valid v4 hook address with `BEFORE_INITIALIZE`, report ERC-165 support for `IInitializerHook`, and return the exact LBP strategy from `authorized()`. This lets only that strategy initialize the committed pool. A dynamic-fee pool requires a nonzero hook ([deployed validation](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/873cbb23c5019a795193c5ad561edff2f78ba5a3/src/libraries/MigratorParams.sol#L133-L157), [technical reference](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/1eda9f0c0243e2fdc0cbe0d665200ffa8c2ba53a/docs/TechnicalReference.md#lbp-hook-requirement)). ORBIT's `CanonicalFeeHook` must be adapted and redeployed to satisfy this initializer interface and address-bit constraint; an arbitrary existing hook cannot be supplied.

For a static-fee `hook = address(0)` launch, stock LBP prefers the hookless pool but switches at migration to a pool using the LBP strategy itself as hook if the hookless key is already initialized. Consumers must read the emitted pool key. This path cannot provide ORBIT's 3% WETH-side accounting or canonical-router checks ([deployment guide](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/1eda9f0c0243e2fdc0cbe0d665200ffa8c2ba53a/docs/DeploymentGuide.md#static-fee-launches-with-hook--address0-hookless-pool-with-strategy-hooked-fallback)).

Use WETH, not native ETH, as the CCA currency. CCA supports both, but the migrated pool always pairs FUEL with the auction currency. Native ETH would create a different key and bypass the WETH-side meaning of ORBIT's hook. The SDK's “quick launch” preset uses native ETH and a fixed 1-billion-token, 50/50 shape; it is an optional preset, not a protocol constraint ([current SDK preset](https://github.com/Uniswap/sdks/blob/35c4e35aca9e22169ce17d7106e7fc5f27ccd03d/sdks/liquidity-launcher-sdk/src/quickLaunch.ts#L35-L143)).

### LP custody and locking

Stock LBP does not lock LP positions by itself. It mints v4 PositionManager NFTs to caller-selected recipients ([migration plan](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/873cbb23c5019a795193c5ad561edff2f78ba5a3/src/strategies/lbp/LBPStrategy.sol#L272-L316)). Uniswap's `TimelockedPositionRecipient` eventually lets an operator transfer the NFT, so it is not intrinsically permanent. The documented irrecoverable `FeeSplitter` is restricted to native-ETH pairs, so it is not a stock permanent custodian for FUEL/WETH ([periphery summary](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/1eda9f0c0243e2fdc0cbe0d665200ffa8c2ba53a/docs/TechnicalReference.md#periphery-contracts), [`TimelockedPositionRecipient`](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/1eda9f0c0243e2fdc0cbe0d665200ffa8c2ba53a/src/periphery/TimelockedPositionRecipient.sol)). ORBIT needs a WETH-compatible terminal position recipient, or an explicit and tested NFT burn/custody construction, if permanent locking remains required.

## Actionable testnet plan

1. Explicitly revise the hackathon economics: choose the FUEL auction supply, reserved LP amount, positive WETH allocation rate, unsold-token policy, and recovery recipient. If the all-4,444 one-sided invariant is retained, stop using stock LBP and scope a custom `IStrategy`; the launcher is modular, but that custom strategy must still reconcile CCA claims and refunds ([strategy interface and modularity](https://github.com/Uniswap/liquidity-launcher/blob/1eda9f0c0243e2fdc0cbe0d665200ffa8c2ba53a/docs/TechnicalReference.md#distribution-strategies)).
2. Redeploy `CanonicalFeeHook` with `InitializerHook` compatibility and mine the required permission bits. Register the exact FUEL/WETH/fee/tick-spacing/hook pool key. Add a WETH-compatible terminal LP recipient.
3. Implement a deterministic bidder-escrow factory plus CCA validation hook. Make the escrow the bid owner, bind it to the funding user, account for WETH refunds and FUEL fills, and cap each FUEL release at the actual mutation budget (at most 64 whole-unit changes, lower when the sender side is non-exempt).
4. Predict all addresses, mark every FUEL-holding system endpoint exempt, and atomically deposit/distribute FUEL while it is still unlaunched. Run a short WETH-denominated CCA with at least successful, outbid/partial-refund, and failed-auction fixtures.
5. Exit bids and call `LBPStrategy.migrate` before `FuelCore.launch()`. The current registry authorizes PoolManager FUEL settlement in the active canonical-swap path, so a post-launch PositionManager migration is expected to fail. Sweep unsold FUEL to its exempt policy recipient and verify exact conservation before launch.
6. Launch FUEL only after successful pool migration and custody validation. Then permissionlessly claim into exempt escrows and release to ordinary wallets in bounded transactions, verifying one collectible/discovery unit per whole FUEL and the permanent-burn path.
7. On Base Sepolia, pin the actual contract addresses, source revisions, auction parameters, receipts, clearing price, migration event/pool key, LP owner, failed-auction refunds, unsold balance, and bounded claims in the deployment manifest and submission evidence.

Unresolved checks before implementation are deliberately narrow: confirm Base Sepolia bytecode and `initializerFactory()` against the SDK; prove the adapted hook's initialization callback coexists with canonical-router enforcement; choose the permanent WETH LP custodian; and decide whether changing the all-supply one-sided genesis invariant is acceptable for the hackathon build.
