# Preserve the 4,444-unit collection and 3% market fee

The primary protocol will adapt QUOTRONS' core economy to Base's four canonical stock tracks while retaining 4,444 economic units: 4,440 ordinary collectibles distributed symmetrically as 1,110 per track, plus four basket-wide Relics. Each track contains 612 Tier I collectibles at 1x weight, 333 Tier II at 1.5x, 133 Tier III at 2.5x, and 32 Tier IV at 5x. The Canonical Market will charge 3% of WETH-side volume, allocating 2% to Stock Rewards, 0.85% to permanently locked Protocol-Owned Liquidity, and 0.15% to the creator. This preserves QUOTRONS' total fee, reward rate, and creator allocation while redirecting the omitted STONK BROKERS allocation into market depth. Following QUOTRONS' verified design, each liquidity cycle deposits only queued WETH into a one-sided Uniswap v4 range and permanently locks the position; it neither buys Liquid Tokens first nor exposes a liquidity-removal path.

Each completed Reward Track conversion follows QUOTRONS' intrinsic collection split: 82.5% for ordinary Permanent Collectibles according to tier weight, 12.5% divided equally among the three Basket Relics, and 5% for the Indicator Relic. Relic allocations accrue to their identities before Commitment but cannot be claimed until those identities become permanent.

If a track has no ordinary Permanent Collectibles, its 82.5% ordinary share accumulates as an Unclaimed Track Pot. The first ordinary collectible committed to that track receives the complete pot; subsequent inflows are shared among all active ordinary collectibles according to tier weight. This preserves QUOTRONS' first-commitment jackpot rather than retroactively crediting every dark identity.
